Field manual

Filing a grudge

  1. Open File. Pick the accused, or choose "New person" to open their dossier on the spot.
  2. Write the charge and, if you like, the full account.
  3. Set the hour it happened. Everything is kept to the hour.
  4. Turn the severity knob (1 to 100). Each step clicks; letting go locks it in and the phone buzzes.
  5. Pick a statute of limitations. The clock starts at the hour of the offense.
  6. Press Enter into the ledger, then press and hold the red button until it fills. Letting go early cancels.

Countdown to peace

Peace arrives when the last outstanding grudge expires. One eternal grudge means peace never comes until you forgive or settle it.

Closing a case

  • Forgive drops the grudge early.
  • Settled means the matter was dealt with.
  • Strike deletes the entry as if it were never filed.
  • When a statute runs out, the grudge closes itself as Statute lapsed.

Standing

Each person's standing is the total severity of their open grudges: In Good Standing (0), On Notice (1+), Under Review (50+), Persona Non Grata (150+), Excommunicated (300+). Every change is written to their dossier's history.
